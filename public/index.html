Frozen Lake 4x4

          
Description: Navigate frozen lake without falling in holes.

          
Start: S0 (Top-left)

          
Goal: S15 (Bottom-right) - Reward: +1

          
Holes: S5, S7, S11, S12 - Reward: -1

          
Monte Carlo Control Algorithms:

          
    
            
  • First Visit: Only first occurrence of state-action in episode counts. Less variance.
    • 
            
  • Every Visit: All occurrences of state-action in episode count. More data per episode.
    • 
          

          
Recommended Parameters:

          
    
            
  • Episodes: 10000+ for Monte Carlo, 1000-5000 for TD
    • 
            
  • Gamma: 0.95-0.99
    • 
            
  • Epsilon: Start at 0.1-0.3, decays over time
    • 
            
  • Alpha: 0.1-0.3 for TD methods (SARSA, Q-learning)
